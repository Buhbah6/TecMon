# TecMon Credits

Thanks to everyone who helped build, shape, and support TecMon.

## Special Thanks

TecMon is a project spearheaded by Mr. Nadeau and inspired by the iTec program at Heritage Regional High School.
The game could not exist without the incredible current and former iTec students who took time out of their summer to work on it.
From assets, to core gameplay mechanics, to design input, the project is a huge collaborative effort.

Also, huge thanks to the iTec team for all the work that goes into the program.
